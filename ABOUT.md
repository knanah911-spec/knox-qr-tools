# About KNOX

KNOX QR Tools is a small, simple browser utility for creating and reading QR codes. The goal is to make everyday QR tasks accessible without an account or a dedicated app installation.

## Our principles
- **Simplicity:** focused tools with a straightforward interface.
- **Privacy-conscious design:** QR image decoding is performed in the browser, not by an app-specific backend.
- **Transparency:** project files are available for inspection and reuse under the MIT License.
- **Accessibility:** responsive layout and English/Arabic interface.

## Project
**KNOX QR Tools** lets you create a QR code from a URL, extract a link from a QR image, or decode QR content as text.
